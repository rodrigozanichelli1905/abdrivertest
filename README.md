AB Driver Test ("the App") is developed to help users prepare for the
Alberta Class 7 Learner's Licence knowledge test. This Privacy Policy
explains how the App handles information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information
on external servers. The App does not use any analytics, advertising,
or tracking services.

The following data is stored locally on your device only, and is never
sent anywhere:
  - Your selected language preference
  - Your practice exam attempt history (scores and answers)
  - Whether you have acknowledged the in-app disclaimer

This data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time by
uninstalling the App or clearing its local storage.

2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising,
or data-collection services. The App does not require an internet
connection to function.

3. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect information at all.

4. DATA SHARING

Since no data is collected or transmitted, no data is shared with any
third party, including the developer.

5. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will
be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

6. DISCLAIMER

This App is for practice purposes only and is not affiliated with the
Government of Alberta or any official licensing authority. Always
refer to the official Alberta Driver's Guide for accurate information.
